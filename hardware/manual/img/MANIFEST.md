# Assembly image manifest

Captions describe visible actions. Most instructions and check/cross marks belong in the HTML; relay_complete_labeled and the requested LCD pod views intentionally embed callouts. The LCD wiring image shows an unpowered mechanical fit using the approved 3.3 V-only plan.

Only the processor heatsink is installed in these pictures. Breadboard row 1 is nearest the Pi; holes a–e face the header/FINGER wall, and f–j face the USB-C wall.

The current build has no OLED or short Qwiic cable. Four exterior finger-joint glue dots secure the sensor cradle. Two lid posts retain the Pi on its locating pegs without glue. Two straps secure the lid, box and bank; two narrow rubber bands retain the bodycam plate. The 500 mm finger cable connects directly to the SHIM. The bank, straight USB-C cable and black straps are generic examples, supplied by the builder.

Printed shapes come from the no-snap CAD. Rigid locators align the parts; glue and bands retain them. Reference components and flexible cable routes are simplified.

The parts sheet shows A, B, C, D, F, G, T1 and T2. The optional rail-fit test is pictured separately. Label anchors are in [parts_printed.json](parts_printed.json). Re-render AST images from hardware/ with `manual/src/render_all.sh`.
