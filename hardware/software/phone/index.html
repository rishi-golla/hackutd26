3. Field-of-view check (optional)

Compression-rate test

  1. Turn the metronome on, put the phone in the enclosure on the chest harness.
  2. Kneel and compress a firm cushion at the beat (~110/min). Arms straight, as in CPR.
  3. The rate should settle at 100–120 within ~3 s. Try slow (~80) and fast (~140) too.
  4. Stop for more than 10 s: "PAUSE" must appear.
